# Research

Studies written before and during the build, kept as they were apart from redactions. They record what was known
and believed at the time; several conclusions were later overturned by measurement (the engine's measured numbers
are in `docs/`).

| folder | contents |
|---|---|
| `reports/` | the main pre-build studies: streaming-engine plan and its adversarial review, performance techniques, the 40 tok/s frontier, the end-to-end plan for firmware 13.00 |
| `notes/` | the source notes behind each report (literature, community benchmarks, loaders, GFX1013) |
| `design-history/` | superseded plans and specs (see its README) |

The reports were written for the project team and sometimes address the reader directly.
